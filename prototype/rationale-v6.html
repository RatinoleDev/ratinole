Questions people ask first.

Can the agent take my money?

No. The vault contract only lets the agent swap between approved tokens inside the vault. Withdrawals need your wallet.

Will it make a profit?

Nobody can promise that. What you get is a loss limit the contract enforces, a backtest scored on unseen days, and a public record of every decision.

What does "reasoning" mean here?

The agent gathers several kinds of evidence, scores them for and against the trade, and writes down why it acted or waited. A rule bot checks one indicator.

What can it trade?

ETH, BTC and tokenized US stocks against USDG on Robinhood Chain. Paper mode covers more markets, including perpetuals.

What happens if Rationale goes offline?

Your vault keeps working without us. You can withdraw directly from the contract on the block explorer.
